PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Aluminum Square Tube Radius Corner 6061-T6 exhibits above average corrosion resistance, good machinability, high strength, light weight, good weldability, and the ability to be heat treated for even higher strength, all at an affordable price. 6061 is the most commonly used aluminum alloy in the world due to its positive characteristics. It is typically used in aerospace, automotive, cameras, couplings, marine hardware, electrical components, decorative hardware, structural applications, pins, brakes, pistons, valves, and bicycle frames.
Tube is designed to act as structural support, whereas Pipe is designed to carry liquid/gas/material from one place to another.

FAQs
Can I order custom cut lengths for the 1 1/2" x 1/8" Aluminum Square Tube 6061-T6?
Yes, this aluminum square tube is available for custom cutting at the warehouse, with lengths up to 240 inches. You can select from various standard cut lengths or request a specific size.
Is the 6061-T6 aluminum square tube suitable for structural support applications?
Absolutely. The 6061-T6 aluminum square tube is designed for structural support, offering high strength, good weldability, and corrosion resistance, making it ideal for aerospace, automotive, marine hardware, and other structural uses.
What are the corrosion resistance and machinability characteristics of this 6061-T6 aluminum tube?
This 6061-T6 aluminum square tube exhibits above average corrosion resistance and good machinability, making it a versatile and durable choice for various projects.
Is this aluminum square tube compliant with RoHS and REACH standards?
Yes, the 6061-T6 aluminum square tube complies with both RoHS and REACH regulations, ensuring it meets environmental and safety standards.
